-   **2% Extra Credit**: Explain why password salts are useful, even if
    they\'re not secret.
    A salt is a random value stored alongside the password hash. Even though it is public, a unique salt makes identical passwords 
    produce different hashes and prevents attackers from reusing precomputed hash tables. It also means each password hash must be 
    attacked separately. Salts don’t make weak passwords strong, but they make large-scale cracking less efficient.
